## How to apply

1. Read a couple of the lab's recent [papers](/publications/) and decide which [research direction](/research/) interests you.
2. Email [Maryam Ramezani](mailto:maryam.ramezani@sharif.edu) with your CV, transcript, and a short paragraph on what you want to work on and why.
3. Include links to code you have written, if you have any.

We read every message. If you have not heard back within two weeks, it is fine to write again.

## What we look for

- You can turn an idea into working code without being told how.
- You are curious about both sides of the lab: how models work, and how the systems around them work.
- You write clearly, and you are honest about what did not work.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to have published before?** No. Most students join without a publication. We care more about what you can build and how you think about a problem.

**Can I work with the lab if I am not a Sharif student?** For MSc and PhD, you first need to be admitted to the Department of Computer Engineering. For short research internships, see the internship entry above.

**How much time does an undergraduate project take?** Roughly ten hours a week for at least two terms.

## Our commitment

The lab is a place where everyone is treated with respect, whatever their background, gender or beliefs. Disagreement about ideas is welcome; disrespect towards people is not.
