## Getting here

The Department of Computer Engineering is on the Sharif University of Technology campus on Azadi Avenue. The nearest metro station is **Sharif University** on Line 2. Visitors without a Sharif card should arrange their visit by email beforehand, so that we can meet you at the entrance.
