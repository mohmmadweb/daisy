abstract: >-
  Health Technology Assessment (HTA) is a crucial tool for evaluating health technologies
  and providing evidence-based guidance for their adoption. This study explores the
  opportunities and challenges of using artificial intelligence in HTA, with a specific
  focus on economic dimensions, following Arksey and O'Malley's scoping review framework
  over PubMed, Scopus and Web of Science. AI enhances HTA outcomes by driving
  methodological advancement, improving utility and enabling comprehensive assessment
  through robust data systems; ethical considerations such as bias, transparency and
  accountability require deliberate planning and policymaking for responsible integration.

A scoping review of how artificial intelligence is used in health technology assessment, and what it costs to use it responsibly — with the economic dimension of HTA as the focus.
