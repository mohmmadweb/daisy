## Before submission

- Share a full draft with your supervisor at least two weeks before the deadline.
- Release code and data with the camera-ready version whenever possible.

## Adding a paper to the website

Create a file in `content/publications/` (copy `_template.md`), fill in the fields, and commit. The site rebuilds in about two minutes.
