This handbook collects how the lab works. It is a sample — edit it to match the lab's real practice.

## First week

1. Send the director your photo and a two-sentence bio so you can be added to the People page.
2. Get access to the lab's shared drive, chat group and compute accounts.
3. Read the three most recent papers in your research direction.

## Weekly rhythm

- One-to-one meeting with your supervisor.
- Lab meeting: each week one member presents progress or a paper.
- Reading group: see the Events page for the schedule.
