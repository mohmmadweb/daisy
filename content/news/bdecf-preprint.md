title: Uncertainty-aware recommendation with Bayesian deep ensembles
date: 2025-04-14
category: publication
summary: BDECF combines Bayesian neural networks with deep ensembles so a recommender can report how certain it is, not only what it predicts.
people: [maryam-ramezani]
publication: bdecf-uncertainty-recommendation
tags: [Recommender Systems, Uncertainty]
---

Recommenders trained on sparse explicit feedback are most confident exactly where they have the least evidence. BDECF carries epistemic uncertainty in its weights and aggregates an ensemble, so disagreement between members becomes a usable signal.
